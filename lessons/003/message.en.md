🇪🇸 *Lesson 3 — Language Transfer*

*Key pattern:*
Spanish shares thousands of words with English through Latin\. Many English words ending in \-ant or \-ent become Spanish by adding an _e_: important becomes _importante_\. Words with a j\-sound \(not the letter, the sound\) turn that into a soft Spanish g, written as _g_ or _j_: general becomes _general_, original becomes _original_\. Words ending in \-ble work the same way: possible becomes _posible_\. The stress falls on the second\-to\-last syllable\.

*New constructions:*
\- _es_ — is \(he/she/it is\)
\- _importante_ — important
\- _diferente_ — different
\- _constante_ — constant
\- _constantemente_ — constantly
\- _legalmente_ — legally
\- _ilegalmente_ — illegally
\- _real_ — real \(also royal\)
\- _realmente_ — really
\- _normal_ — normal
\- _normalmente_ — normally
\- _general_ — general
\- _generalmente_ — generally
\- _original_ — original
\- _originalmente_ — originally
\- _digital_ — digital
\- _naturalmente_ — naturally
\- _posible_ — possible
\- _posiblemente_ — possibly
\- _probable_ — probable
\- _probablemente_ — probably
\- _flexible_ — flexible

*Remember:*
Convertible words give you over 3,000 Spanish words immediately\. Some rules have exceptions, but experimenting freely — and being comfortable with errors — is what lets you learn quickly\.

*Examples:*
\- _Es importante_ — It's important
\- _No es importante_ — It's not important
\- _No es diferente_ — It's not different
\- _Es constante_ — It is constant
\- _Normalmente, es importante_ — Normally, it's important
\- _Es original_ — It's original
\- _Naturalmente, es original_ — Naturally, it's original
\- _Es posible_ — It's possible
