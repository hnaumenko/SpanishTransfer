🇪🇸 *Lesson 6 — Language Transfer*

*The core rule:*
You can often figure out Spanish verbs by thinking of English words that come from the same Latin root\. _Intento_ isn't built from a rule, but if you think of "intention," you can hear what it means: I try, or I'm trying\. The same logic works for many other verbs — just look for the Latin cousin hiding in English\.

*New constructions:*
\- _intento_ — I try / I'm trying
\- _voy_ — I go / I'm going \(related to "voyage"; spelled with V but pronounced like a soft B\)
\- _visitar_ — to visit
\- _ahora_ — now \(literally "to the hour"; the H is silent\)
\- _a_ — to \(in the sense of direction\)
\- _hora_ — hour
\- _vamos_ — let's go / we're going

*Remember:*
The H in Spanish is completely silent — even in your mind\. If you think it, it tends to slip out\. Words like _hospital_ should sound like they start with O: _ospital_\.

*Examples:*
\- _Intento publicarlo\._ — I'm trying to publish it\.
\- _Intento imitarlo\._ — I'm trying to imitate it \(or him\)\.
\- _Quiero cancelarlo, pero no quiero cancelarlo ahora\._ — I want to cancel it, but I don't want to cancel it now\.
\- _Voy ahora\._ — I'm going now\.
\- _No voy ahora\._ — I'm not going now\.
\- _Quiero visitarlo\._ — I want to visit him\.
