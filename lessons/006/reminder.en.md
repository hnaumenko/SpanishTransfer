🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that many Spanish verbs can be guessed by thinking of related English words from the same Latin root, and that the letter H in Spanish is completely silent\.
Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I'm trying to cancel it\.
👉 ||Intento cancelarlo\.||

2️⃣ I want to visit him now\.
👉 ||Quiero visitarlo ahora\.||

3️⃣ I'm not going now\.
👉 ||No voy ahora\.||

4️⃣ I'm trying to imitate it\.
👉 ||Intento imitarlo\.||

5️⃣ I want to publish it, but I'm not going now\.
👉 ||Quiero publicarlo, pero no voy ahora\.||
