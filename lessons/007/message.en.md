🇪🇸 *Lesson 7 — Language Transfer*

*Key pattern:*
When a verb of movement like _voy_ \(I'm going\) or _pasar_ \(to pass by\) connects to another verb, Spanish adds an extra _a_ to show the sense of motion\. So you say _voy a continuar_ — literally "I'm going to to continue"\. English doesn't do this, but Spanish wants that motion visible even when you're just talking about the future\.

*New constructions:*
\- _continuar_ — to continue
\- _intento_ — I try / I'm trying
\- _demostrar_ — to demonstrate
\- _mostrar_ — to show \(from _demostrar_ without the _de\-_\)
\- _algo_ — something
\- _voy_ — I'm going / I go
\- _los_ — them \(plural of _lo_\)
\- _ver_ — to see
\- _pasar_ — to pass / to pass by

*Remember:*
When your brain locks on "do I remember it?", the game is over\. Instead, ask yourself what the word was related to or what it sounded like — then see what pops up\.

*Examples:*
\- _Intento continuar_ — I'm trying to continue
\- _Voy a confiscarlo_ — I'm going to confiscate it
\- _Quiero verlos_ — I want to see them
\- _Voy a intentar verlos_ — I'm going to try to see them
\- _Voy a pasar a visitarte_ — I'm going to pass by to visit you
\- _Quiero mostrarte algo_ — I want to show you something
\- _Quiero pasar a mostrarte algo_ — I want to pass by to show you something
