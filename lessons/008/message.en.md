🇪🇸 *Lesson 8 — Language Transfer*

*Main idea:*
Spanish uses _la_ for feminine "it," "her," and formal "you" \(female\)\. Just as _lo_ covers masculine meanings, _la_ handles the feminine equivalents\. The word doesn't change based on whether you're talking about a person or a thing — what matters is whether the Spanish word itself is masculine or feminine\. A house is _la casa_, so "I want to see it" uses _la_\.

*New constructions:*
\- _la_ — her, it \(feminine\), you \(formal, female\)
\- _las_ — them \(feminine\), you plural \(formal, female\)
\- _venir_ — to come
\- _debo_ — I owe, I must
\- _por qué_ — why

*Remember:*
The formal _la_ and _lo_ follow the same pattern as verbs like _es_ and _quiere_ — they work for he, she, it, and formal you all at once\.

*Examples:*
\- _Quiero verla_ — I want to see her
\- _¿Quiere visitarme?_ — Do you want to visit me?
\- _¿Por qué no quiere venir a la casa?_ — Why don't you want to come to the house?
\- _Debo continuar_ — I must continue
\- _Debo identificarlos_ — I must identify them
\- _Debo identificarlas_ — I must identify them \(feminine\)
\- _¿Quiere venir a visitarme?_ — Do you want to come to visit me?
