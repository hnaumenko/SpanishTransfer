🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that *la* means "her," "it" \(for feminine nouns\), and formal "you" \(female\), while *las* is the plural form, and we also picked up "to come," "I must," and "why\."

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I want to see her\.
👉 ||Quiero verla\.||

2️⃣ Why don't you want to come?
👉 ||¿Por qué no quiere venir?||

3️⃣ I must continue\.
👉 ||Debo continuar\.||

4️⃣ Do you want to come to visit me?
👉 ||¿Quiere venir a visitarme?||

5️⃣ I must identify them \(feminine\)\.
👉 ||Debo identificarlas\.||
