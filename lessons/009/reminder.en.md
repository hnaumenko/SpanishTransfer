🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that _saber_ means "to know," _si_ means "if" \(and "yes"\), and you can build long sentences by focusing on one simple block at a time\.
Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I don't want to know\.
👉 ||No quiero saber\.||

2️⃣ I want to know if she wants to come\.
👉 ||Quiero saber si quiere venir\.||

3️⃣ Why doesn't he want to visit me?
👉 ||¿Por qué no quiere visitarme?||

4️⃣ I want to explain something to you\.
👉 ||Quiero explicarte algo\.||

5️⃣ I'm not trying to justify myself, but I want to know if he wants to come to the house\.
👉 ||No intento justificarme, pero quiero saber si quiere venir a la casa\.||
