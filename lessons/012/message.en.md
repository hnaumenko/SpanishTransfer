🇪🇸 *Lesson 12 — Language Transfer*

*Main idea:*
Verbs that end in \-er have a different key vowel than \-ar verbs\. While \-ar verbs use "a" as their key sound, \-er verbs use "e"\. You still follow the same pattern: take off the ending, add the key vowel for he/she/it/you formal, then add "n" for they/you plural or "s" for you informal\. This pattern with "n" and "s" appears throughout all Spanish verbs\.

*New constructions:*
\- _vender_ — to sell
\- _vendo_ — I sell / I'm selling
\- _vende_ — he/she/it sells, you formal sell
\- _venden_ — they sell, you plural sell
\- _vendes_ — you informal sell
\- _nos_ — us
\- _algo_ — something

*Remember:*
When you're building a sentence with pronouns like "it" and "me," the personal pronoun always comes first\. So "they sell it to me" becomes _me lo venden_ \- first me, then it\.

*Examples:*
\- _Vendo algo_ — I'm selling something
\- _Lo vende_ — He sells it
\- _¿Por qué no me lo venden?_ — Why don't they sell it to me?
\- _Nos venden algo_ — They're selling us something
\- _No nos lo venden_ — They're not selling it to us
\- _¿Por qué no nos lo venden?_ — Why don't they sell it to us?
