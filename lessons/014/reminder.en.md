🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that _saber_ \(to know\) is irregular in the "I" form \(_sé_\), and that _tengo que_ means "I have to" while pronouns like _él_ and _ella_ are optional since verb endings show who's doing the action\.
Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I don't know what she wants\.
👉 ||No sé qué quiere\.||

2️⃣ I have to see him\.
👉 ||Tengo que verlo\.||

3️⃣ When are you leaving?
👉 ||¿Cuándo sales?||

4️⃣ I suppose so\.
👉 ||Supongo que sí\.||

5️⃣ She's going out to eat\.
👉 ||Sale a comer\.||
