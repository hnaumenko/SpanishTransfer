🇪🇸 *Lesson 17 — Language Transfer*

*What this lesson is about:*
The "we" form of verbs is made by taking off the _\-ar_ ending and adding _\-amos_\. So _hablar_ \(to speak\) becomes _hablamos_ \(we speak\)\. This pattern is just like the other forms you've learned — you remove the infinitive ending and add the new one\. The accent in the "we" form falls on the second\-to\-last syllable, which means the stress moves away from the verb stem\. This is important because it affects whether the vowel splits\.

*New constructions:*
\- _tomar_ — to take, or to drink
\- _alcohol_ — alcohol \(from Arabic _al\-kohl_; the _al\-_ means "the"\)
\- _azúcar_ / _athúcar_ — sugar \(from Arabic; the _a\-_ comes from _al\-_ assimilated\)
\- _arroz_ — rice \(from Arabic _al\-ruz_; the double _r_ shows the rolling sound\)
\- _álgebra_ — algebra
\- _colgar_ — to hang
\- _\-amos_ — the ending for "we" in the present tense with _\-ar_ verbs

*Remember:*
When you add _\-amos_ to make the "we" form, the stress shifts to the _a_ in _\-amos_\. That means the _e_ or _o_ in the verb stem is no longer stressed, so it doesn't split\. The vowel splitting only happens when you put pressure on it\.

*Examples:*
\- _Hablamos_ — We speak
\- _Tomamos arroz_ — We eat rice
\- _No tomamos alcohol_ — We don't drink alcohol
\- _Encontramos_ — We find
\- _Pensamos_ — We think
\- _Cerramos_ — We close
\- _¿Qué pensamos hacer?_ — What do we plan to do?
\- _Mostramos_ — We show
