🇪🇸 *Lesson 18 — Language Transfer*

*The core rule:*
Spanish verbs split into three groups when we talk about "we": \-_ar_ verbs take \-_amos_, \-_er_ verbs take \-_emos_, and \-_ir_ verbs take \-_imos_\. This is the only place in the entire language where \-_ar_ and \-_er_ verbs behave differently\. Everywhere else, they follow the same pattern\. For \-_ir_ verbs, the _i_ from the ending appears in the conjugation for the first time in the present tense\.

*New constructions:*
\- _preferir_ — to prefer
\- _preferimos_ — we prefer
\- _venir_ — to come
\- _venimos_ — we come
\- _vivir_ — to live
\- _vivimos_ — we live
\- _aquí / acá_ — here \(_aquí_ in Spain, _acá_ in Latin America\)
\- _saber_ — to know
\- _sabemos_ — we know
\- _dónde_ — where
\- _entender_ — to understand
\- _entendemos_ — we understand
\- _perder_ — to lose
\- _perdemos_ — we lose
\- _siempre_ — always

*Remember:*
Pronouncing words the way people around you pronounce them makes understanding easier\. The soft _b_ sound appears for both _b_ and _v_ in Spanish\.

*Examples:*
\- _Prefiero vivir aquí_ — I prefer to live here
\- _Venimos siempre_ — We always come
\- _Vivo aquí_ — I live here
\- _Él vive aquí_ — He lives here
\- _No entiendo dónde viven_ — I don't understand where they live
\- _Sabemos dónde vive_ — We know where he lives
\- _Siempre perdemos_ — We always lose
\- _¿Por qué no entiende?_ — Why doesn't he understand?
