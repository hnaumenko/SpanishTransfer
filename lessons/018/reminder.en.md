🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned how "we" forms work: \-ar verbs take \-amos, \-er verbs take \-emos, and \-ir verbs take \-imos — this is the only place where \-ar and \-er verbs behave differently\.

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ We prefer to live here\.
👉 ||Preferimos vivir aquí\.||

2️⃣ We always come\.
👉 ||Venimos siempre\.||

3️⃣ We know where he lives\.
👉 ||Sabemos dónde vive\.||

4️⃣ I don't understand where they live\.
👉 ||No entiendo dónde viven\.||

5️⃣ Why doesn't he understand?
👉 ||¿Por qué no entiende?||
