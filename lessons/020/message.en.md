🇪🇸 *Lesson 20 — Language Transfer*

*Main idea:*
Spanish uses the present tense to talk about the future in two ways\. You can use _voy a_ \(I'm going to\) followed by a verb, just like in English\. Or you can simply use the present tense with future context words like _pronto_ \(soon\), _mañana_ \(tomorrow\), or _la semana que viene_ \(next week\)\. The context itself tells the listener you're talking about the future\.

*New constructions:*
\- _pronto_ — soon
\- _veo_ — I see \(irregular first person of _ver_\)
\- _nos_ — us, ourselves, each other
\- _nos vemos_ — we see each other \(a way to say goodbye\)
\- _la semana_ — the week
\- _la semana que viene_ — the week that comes, next week

*Remember:*
When you forget a word, you don't need to just remember it\. You need to rebuild it using the process you learned\. Every time you do this, you strengthen the process itself, which matters more than memorizing individual words\.

*Examples:*
\- _La veo mañana_ — I'm seeing her tomorrow
\- _La vemos pronto_ — We're seeing her soon
\- _¿Vas a verlo pronto?_ — Are you going to see him soon?
\- _¿Vas a vernos pronto?_ — Are you going to see us soon?
\- _¿No vas a vernos?_ — Aren't you going to see us?
\- _Hablamos la semana que viene_ — Let's speak next week
\- _Nos vemos la semana que viene_ — We'll see each other next week
