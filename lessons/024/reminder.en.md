🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that *dar* \(to give\) follows regular patterns, and when saying "give it to me," the personal pronoun \(me, te, nos\) comes before lo/la\.

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ They give me something\.
👉 ||Me dan algo\.||

2️⃣ I'll give it to you tomorrow\.
👉 ||Te lo doy mañana\.||

3️⃣ Are you giving it to me tomorrow?
👉 ||¿Me lo da mañana?||

4️⃣ I want to give it to you\.
👉 ||Quiero dártelo\.||

5️⃣ Will you guys give it to us tomorrow?
👉 ||¿Ustedes nos lo dan mañana?||
