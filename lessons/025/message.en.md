🇪🇸 *Lesson 25 — Language Transfer*

*What this lesson is about:*
Spanish doesn't need words like "to," "for," "from," or "about" as often as English does\. When you say _me habla_, the _me_ already covers "to me," "for me," or "from me" depending on context\. English forces you to spell out these relationships, but Spanish lets the context do the work\. You also learn how to say "I stay" by combining "remain" with the reflexive — literally "I remain myself\."

*New constructions:*
\- _comprar_ — to buy
\- _hacer_ — to do \(irregular verb\)
\- _la cocina_ — the kitchen
\- _cocinar_ — to cook
\- _algo_ — something
\- _ocho_ — eight
\- _nueve_ — nine
\- _diez_ — ten
\- _quedar_ — to remain, to be left over
\- _me quedo_ — I stay \(literally "I remain myself"\)
\- _hasta_ — until
\- _hasta la vista_ — until we see each other

*Remember:*
The reflexive pronoun isn't optional when you use _quedar_ to mean "stay\." You must say _me quedo_, _te quedas_, _nos quedamos_ — the reflexive is what creates the meaning of staying, not just remaining\.

*Examples:*
\- _Me habla_ — He speaks to me
\- _Me hablan de ello_ — They speak to me about it
\- _Me lo compra_ — He buys it for me
\- _¿Te lo compro?_ — Shall I buy it for you?
\- _¿Puedes cocinar algo para mí?_ — Can you cook something for me?
\- _He cocinado a las seis_ — I cooked at six
\- _Me quedo aquí hasta las diez_ — I'm staying here until ten
\- _¿Te quedas aquí?_ — Are you staying here?
