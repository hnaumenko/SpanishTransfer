🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned how to introduce yourself using the reflexive form of "llamar" — "me llamo" literally means "I call myself," and it's the natural way to say your name in Spanish\.
Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ What's your name? \(informal\)
👉 ||¿Cómo te llamas?||

2️⃣ I'm going to stay until seven\.
👉 ||Voy a quedarme hasta las siete\.||

3️⃣ My name is Ana \(literally: I call myself Ana\)\.
👉 ||Me llamo Ana\.||

4️⃣ Do you want to stay?
👉 ||¿Quieres quedarte?||

5️⃣ I have stayed\.
👉 ||Me he quedado\.||
