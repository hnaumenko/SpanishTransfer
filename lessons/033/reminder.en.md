🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that some adjectives change meaning completely depending on whether you use _ser_ or _estar_ — like _listo_ \(smart vs\. ready\) and _bueno_ \(good person vs\. attractive\)\.
Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I am ready\.
👉 ||Estoy listo\.||

2️⃣ He is very smart\.
👉 ||Es muy listo\.||

3️⃣ I am so tired\.
👉 ||Estoy tan cansado\.||

4️⃣ They are good people\.
👉 ||Son buenos\.||

5️⃣ I am happy \(as a state right now\)\.
👉 ||Estoy feliz\.||
