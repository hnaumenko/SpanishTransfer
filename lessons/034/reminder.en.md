🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that Spanish uses the simple present tense for most situations, and saves the *estar \+ \-ando/\-iendo* structure only for emphasizing what's happening right this moment\.

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ We're having breakfast right now\.
👉 ||Estamos desayunando\.||

2️⃣ I'm preparing it tomorrow\.
👉 ||Lo preparo mañana\.||

3️⃣ They're selling it tomorrow\.
👉 ||Lo venden mañana\.||

4️⃣ I'm waiting here\.
👉 ||Estoy esperando aquí\.||

5️⃣ She's eating right now\.
👉 ||Está comiendo\.||
