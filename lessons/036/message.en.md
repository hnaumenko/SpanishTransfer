🇪🇸 *Lesson 36 — Language Transfer*

*Main idea:*
Spanish has a future tense that doesn't require learning any new sounds\. You take the infinitive \(the full verb ending in \-ar, \-er, or \-ir\) and add endings directly to it — and those endings are the same as the forms of _haber_ you already know\. The accent shifts to the ending, pushing it into the future\. Spanish also reflects time through accent placement, just as Greek pulls the accent back into the past\.

*New constructions:*
\- _comeré_ — I will eat
\- _comeremos_ — we will eat
\- _comerá_ — he/she/you formal will eat
\- _comerán_ — they/you guys will eat
\- _comerás_ — you informal will eat
\- _iré_ — I will go
\- _iremos_ — we will go
\- _irá_ — he/she/you formal will go
\- _irán_ — they/you guys will go
\- _irás_ — you informal will go
\- _encontraré_ — I will find
\- _encontraremos_ — we will find
\- _encontrará_ — he/she/you formal will find
\- _quedaré_ — I will remain
\- _prepararé_ — I will prepare
\- _esperaré_ — I will wait
\- _organizará_ — he/she/you formal will organize
\- _organizarán_ — they/you guys will organize
\- _estaré_ — I will be \(from _estar_\)
\- _será_ — it will be \(from _ser_\)

*Remember:*
Think out the complete infinitive first, then add the ending\. A common mistake is dropping the final R of the infinitive because learners don't think through the whole verb before adding the future ending\.

*Examples:*
\- _Comeré con ellos mañana_ — I will eat with them tomorrow
\- _¿Por qué no irás?_ — Why won't you go?
\- _Lo encontraremos_ — We will find it
\- _Me quedaré aquí_ — I will stay here
\- _Prepararé algo_ — I will prepare something
\- _Organizarán la fiesta_ — They will organize the party
\- _Estaré cansado_ — I will be tired
\- _Será interesante_ — It will be interesting
