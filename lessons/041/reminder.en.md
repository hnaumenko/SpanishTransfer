🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned how to form the conditional tense \(would\) by adding _ía_ to the full infinitive, and how Spanish uses reflexive verbs where English says "gets\."

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I would dance\.
👉 ||Bailaría\.||

2️⃣ We would eat later\.
👉 ||Comeríamos más tarde\.||

3️⃣ It would get contaminated\.
👉 ||Se contaminaría\.||

4️⃣ I would go but I don't have time\.
👉 ||Iría pero no tengo tiempo\.||

5️⃣ I would imagine that it would transform the situation\.
👉 ||Imaginaría que transformaría la situación\.||
