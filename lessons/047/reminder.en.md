🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that when an action happens to itself \(something breaks, burns, or sells by itself\), Spanish uses the reflexive structure with *se* instead of naming who's affected\.

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ The dinner was burning\.
👉 ||La cena se quemaba\.||

2️⃣ I get burnt in the sun\.
👉 ||Me quemo en el sol\.||

3️⃣ Those ones break all the time\.
👉 ||Esos se rompen todo el tiempo\.||

4️⃣ He was burning the dinner\.
👉 ||Él quemaba la cena\.||

5️⃣ This car\.
👉 ||Este coche\.||
