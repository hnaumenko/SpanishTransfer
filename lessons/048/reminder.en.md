🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that \-er and \-ir verbs form the past continuous by removing the infinitive ending and adding \-ía, which looks identical to the "would" form but works differently\.

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I was eating\.
👉 ||Comía\.||

2️⃣ It was for sale\.
👉 ||Se vendía\.||

3️⃣ How do you say it?
👉 ||¿Cómo se dice?||

4️⃣ I don't have any left\.
👉 ||No me queda ninguno\.||

5️⃣ We were staying\.
👉 ||Nos quedábamos\.||
