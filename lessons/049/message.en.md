🇪🇸 *Lesson 49 — Language Transfer*

*What this lesson is about:*
This lesson introduces three irregular verbs in the imperfect past tense\. Most verbs follow the regular pattern, but _ver_ \(to see\), _ser_ \(to be\), and _ir_ \(to go\) have their own forms\. The good news is that this tense has very few irregularities overall — it's one of the safest, most predictable tenses in Spanish\.

*New constructions:*
\- _llamar_ — to call \(someone\) or to make a phone call
\- _¿Cómo te llamas?_ — How do you call yourself? \(informal way to ask someone's name\)
\- _contener_ — to contain
\- _obtener_ — to obtain
\- _mantener_ — to maintain
\- _sostener_ — to sustain
\- _cuatro_ — four
\- _ayer_ — yesterday
\- _era_ — I/he/she was \(from _ser_\)
\- _éramos_ — we were
\- _eran_ — they/you\(formal\) were
\- _eras_ — you\(informal\) were
\- _iba_ — I was going \(from _ir_\)
\- _íbamos_ — we were going
\- _iban_ — they/you\(formal\) were going
\- _ibas_ — you\(informal\) were going

*Remember:*
The imperfect forms of _ver_, _ser_, and _ir_ are irregular, but they're short, common words you'll use constantly\. _Era_ is easy to remember because an era is a line in time in the past — just like this tense\.

*Examples:*
\- _¿Cómo se llamaba?_ — What was she called?
\- _Tenía cuatro pero no me quedan\._ — I used to have four but now I don't have any left\.
\- _Tenía que verte ayer\._ — I had to see you yesterday\.
\- _Era estudiante\._ — I used to be a student\.
\- _Éramos estudiantes\._ — We were students\.
\- _Iba a llamarte\._ — I was going to call you\.
\- _Íbamos a hacerlo\._ — We were going to do it\.
\- _Íbamos a hacerlos\._ — We were going to make them\.
