🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that Spanish has two words for "for": *para* means something is literally for someone, while *por* means "because of," is used for time periods, and appears in "why" and "because"\.
Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I'm buying it for you \(it's meant for you\)\.
👉 ||Estoy comprándolo para ti\.||

2️⃣ I'm staying for three days\.
👉 ||Me quedo por tres días\.||

3️⃣ I don't want to go because of this reason\.
👉 ||No quiero ir por esta razón\.||

4️⃣ I work every day\.
👉 ||Trabajo todos los días\.||

5️⃣ I would stay for three days but I have to work\.
👉 ||Me quedaría por tres días pero tengo que trabajar\.||
