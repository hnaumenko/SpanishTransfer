🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that Spanish uses the imperfect tense much more freely than English, so you'll often choose between the two past tenses based on context rather than matching the English form exactly\.

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ People need to eat\.
👉 ||La gente necesita comer\.||

2️⃣ I couldn't do it yesterday\.
👉 ||No podía hacerlo ayer\.||

3️⃣ I needed more time\.
👉 ||Necesitaba más tiempo\.||

4️⃣ It would have been different\.
👉 ||Habría sido diferente\.||

5️⃣ I couldn't do it because I didn't have the time\.
👉 ||No podría hacerlo porque no tenía tiempo\.||
