🇪🇸 *Lesson 65 — Language Transfer*

*What this lesson is about:*
Adding _más_ to negative words creates "else"\. You already know _nada_ \(nothing\) and _nadie_ \(nobody\)\. Put _más_ after them and you get _nada más_ \(nothing else\) and _nadie más_ \(nobody else\)\. This lesson also introduces the possessive words _mi_, _tu_, _su_, and _nuestro_, and shows how they change to match what's being possessed — not who possesses it\.

*New constructions:*
\- _nada más_ — nothing else
\- _nadie más_ — nobody else
\- _vez_ — time \(as in occasion\)
\- _una vez_ — once, one time
\- _una vez más_ — one more time
\- _otra vez_ — again \(literally "other time"\)
\- _nosotros_ — we \(literally "us others"\)
\- _a la vez_ — at the same time
\- _todos_ — everybody
\- _mi casa_ — my house
\- _tu casa_ — your house \(informal\)
\- _su casa_ — his/her/their/your \(formal\) house
\- _nuestra casa_ — our house
\- _propio_ — own
\- _ojo_ — eye

*Remember:*
Possessive words like _mi_, _tu_, and _su_ change to match the number of things being possessed\. If you're talking about one house, it's _su casa_ whether you mean his, her, or their house\. Only say _sus_ if you mean houses — _sus casas_\.

*Examples:*
\- _No comí nada más_ — I didn't eat anything else
\- _Lo vi una vez_ — I saw him once
\- _Quiero verlos otra vez_ — I want to see them again
\- _Llegó tarde a mi casa otra vez_ — He arrived at my house late again
\- _¿A qué hora llegaron a tu casa?_ — At what time did they arrive at your house?
\- _Todos llegaron a mi casa a la vez_ — Everybody arrived at my house at the same time
\- _Estamos con nuestros amigos_ — We are with our friends
\- _Lo vi con mis propios ojos_ — I saw it with my own eyes
