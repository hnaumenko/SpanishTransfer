🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that Spanish uses the subjunctive for commands, except for positive informal commands, which use a shortcut: just drop the _s_ from the tú form\.

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ Don't forget it \(speaking to a friend\)\.
👉 ||No lo olvides\.||

2️⃣ Wait for me \(speaking to several people\)\.
👉 ||Espérenme\.||

3️⃣ Don't stay there \(speaking to a friend\)\.
👉 ||No te quedes ahí\.||

4️⃣ Sell it \(speaking to a friend\)\.
👉 ||Véndelo\.||

5️⃣ Don't eat it \(speaking to several people\)\.
👉 ||No lo coman\.||
