🇪🇸 *Lesson 75 — Language Transfer*

*Key pattern:*
The subjunctive turns question words into "ever" words\. When you take a word like _dónde_ \(where\) or _qué_ \(what\) and use the subjunctive mood with it, you change the meaning from "where" to "wherever," or from "what" to "whatever\." It's a way of opening up possibility rather than pinning something down\. So _donde quieres_ means "where you want," but _donde quieras_ means "wherever you want\."

*New constructions:*
\- _haz_ — informal command from _hacer_ \(irregular: do, make\)
\- _seguir_ — to follow, to continue, or to carry on \(e→i stem change when stressed\)
\- _conseguir_ — to get or to manage to \(from _seguir_\)
\- _perseguir_ — to chase \(from _seguir_\)
\- _sea_ — subjunctive base of _ser_
\- _ojalá_ — I hope, if only \(literally "God willing"\)

*Remember:*
The subjunctive with question words works the same in other contexts too\. _Como quieras_ means "however you like," _cuando necesites_ means "whenever you need\." You're not changing word order or adding extra words — the subjunctive itself carries the shift from specific to open\-ended\.

*Examples:*
\- _Hazlo_ — Do it
\- _Hazlo donde quieras_ — Do it wherever you want
\- _Hazlo como quieras_ — Do it however you like
\- _Come lo que prefieras_ — Eat whatever you prefer
\- _Te sigo a donde vayas_ — I follow you wherever you go
\- _Cuando necesites_ — Whenever you need
\- _Prefiero que sea así_ — I prefer that it be like this
\- _Sea lo que sea_ — Whatever will be will be
