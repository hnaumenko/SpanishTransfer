🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that the subjunctive turns question words into "ever" words — _donde_ becomes "wherever," _cuando_ becomes "whenever," and _como_ becomes "however\."

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ Do it however you like\.
👉 ||Hazlo como quieras\.||

2️⃣ Eat whatever you prefer\.
👉 ||Come lo que prefieras\.||

3️⃣ I follow you wherever you go\.
👉 ||Te sigo a donde vayas\.||

4️⃣ I prefer that it be like this\.
👉 ||Prefiero que sea así\.||

5️⃣ Whenever you need\.
👉 ||Cuando necesites\.||
