🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that Spanish uses *hay* for both "there is" and "there are," and *había* for both "there was" and "there were" — they never change form\. These come from the verb *haber*, so "there has to be" becomes *tiene que haber*\.

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ There is somebody here\.
👉 ||Hay alguien aquí\.||

2️⃣ There were two people here that spoke Spanish\.
👉 ||Había dos personas aquí que hablaban español\.||

3️⃣ Is there somebody here that speaks Spanish?
👉 ||¿Hay alguien aquí que hable español?||

4️⃣ There has to be something that you can do\.
👉 ||Tiene que haber algo que puedas hacer\.||

5️⃣ There are two people here that speak Spanish\.
👉 ||Hay dos personas aquí que hablan español\.||
