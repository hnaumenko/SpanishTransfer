🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that verbs like empezar, comenzar, and terminar need prepositions \(a or de\) before infinitives, but when you use the \-ing form instead, the preposition disappears and the meaning shifts to "start out" or "end up\."

Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I continue speaking\.
👉 ||Sigo hablando\.||

2️⃣ It's imperative that it continues being free\.
👉 ||Es imperativo que siga siendo gratis\.||

3️⃣ It's spectacular that they begin to speak Spanish\.
👉 ||Es espectacular que empiecen a hablar español\.||

4️⃣ It's spectacular that they start out speaking Spanish\.
👉 ||Es espectacular que empiecen hablando español\.||

5️⃣ I want you guys to speak with me when you finish selling them\.
👉 ||Quiero que habléis conmigo cuando terminéis de venderlos\.||
