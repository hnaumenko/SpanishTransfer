🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned how to use *se* with pronouns like *me*, *te*, *nos*, and *les* to show something happened in a way that affects you — the "on me" structure\.
Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ The dinner burnt on me\.
👉 ||Se me quemó la cena\.||

2️⃣ Does the battery run out on you?
👉 ||¿Se te acaba la batería?||

3️⃣ The battery always runs out on me\.
👉 ||La batería siempre se me acaba\.||

4️⃣ The dinner burnt on us\.
👉 ||Se nos quemó la cena\.||

5️⃣ The door closes\.
👉 ||La puerta se cierra\.||
