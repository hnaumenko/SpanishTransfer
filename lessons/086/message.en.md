🇪🇸 *Lesson 86 — Language Transfer*

*The core rule:*
Most "go verbs" — verbs that add _\-go_ in the "I" form of the present tense — contract in the future tenses\. Instead of keeping the full infinitive, they drop the _e_ before the _r_ and replace it with _d_\. So _tener_ becomes _tendr\-_, _venir_ becomes _vendr\-_, _salir_ becomes _saldr\-_\. Once you spot the _go_, you know the verb will likely contract this way in both _will_ and _would_\.

*New constructions:*
\- _tendré_ — I will have \(from _tener_\)
\- _tendría_ — I would have
\- _vendré_ — I will come \(from _venir_\)
\- _vendría_ — I would come
\- _pondré_ — I will put \(from _poner_\)
\- _pondría_ — I would put
\- _saldré_ — I will leave / I will go out \(from _salir_\)
\- _saldría_ — I would leave / I would go out
\- _haré_ — I will do / I will make \(from _hacer_, highly irregular\)
\- _haría_ — I would do / I would make
\- _diré_ — I will say / I will tell \(from _decir_, highly irregular\)
\- _diría_ — I would say / I would tell
\- _podré_ — I will be able to \(from _poder_\)
\- _podría_ — I would be able to / I could
\- _pondré_ — I will put
\- _pondría_ — I would put

*Remember:*
_Hacer_ and _decir_ don't follow the normal go\-verb pattern in the future — they're even more irregular, using _har\-_ and _dir\-_ instead\. But once you know one form, the rest follow the same irregular base\.

*Examples:*
\- _Vendríamos más tarde pero no tenemos tiempo_ — We would come later but we don't have time\.
\- _Lo pondré aquí_ — I will put it here\.
\- _Lo pondría aquí_ — I would put it here\.
\- _¿Saldrás esta noche?_ — Will you go out tonight?
\- _Lo haríamos pero estamos ocupados ahora_ — We would do it but we're busy now\.
\- _Te diré más tarde_ — I will tell you later\.
\- _Pondríamos la mesa_ — We would set the table\.
\- _Podría hacerlo mañana_ — I could do it tomorrow\.
