🌙 *Time to recall your Spanish\!*

Today we learned that most "go verbs" contract in the future tenses by dropping the _e_ before the _r_ and replacing it with _d_, and that _hacer_ and _decir_ are highly irregular using _har\-_ and _dir\-_ instead\.
Translate these phrases in your head, then tap the black rectangles to check yourself:

1️⃣ I will come later\.
👉 ||Vendré más tarde\.||

2️⃣ I would put it here\.
👉 ||Lo pondría aquí\.||

3️⃣ Will you go out tonight?
👉 ||¿Saldrás esta noche?||

4️⃣ I could do it tomorrow\.
👉 ||Podría hacerlo mañana\.||

5️⃣ I will tell you later\.
👉 ||Te diré más tarde\.||
